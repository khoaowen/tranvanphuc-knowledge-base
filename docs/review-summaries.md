# Summary fidelity review

Run `./review-summaries` locally after summaries exist. It invokes `claude -p` (subscription login, never CI); `REVIEW_SUMMARIES_CLAUDE` or `--claude-cmd` can select a compatible executable. `--model sonnet|haiku`, `--limit N`, `--max-retries N`, `--videos-dir PATH`, and `--no-commit` are supported. Each review is committed separately; rerunning skips structurally valid reviews bound to current exact `summary.md` bytes. `--limit` counts only newly completed reviews, not skips.

The reviewer works per claim, not per chunk. `fidelity.extract_claims()` deterministically splits the summary body (after the first `## ` heading; Q: lines, headings, disclaimer and metadata are context only) into sentence-level claims with stable IDs `c001…`. The transcript is split into timestamp-aligned overlapping chunks. Each chunk prompt lists all claims but shows only one slice, and the model reports only claims whose subject appears in that slice, as `in_slice` with `supported`, `attributed`, `qualifications_preserved`, `timestamp_correct`, `contradicts`, a `finding` (required when a check fails) and a verbatim Vietnamese quote. Claims a chunk does not discuss are `not_in_slice` and are never penalised there. Quotes must be verbatim transcript text (case, punctuation and `>>` ignored; a quote may span consecutive captions); quote timestamps are computed by the tool, never taken from the model. Invalid responses are retried (up to 5 times, with the error fed back). Validated chunk results are cached in `videos/<dir>/.review-progress.json` (gitignored, deleted on completion) so a rerun redoes only failed chunks.

Per-claim aggregation (`aggregate_claim`):

- `pass`: at least one chunk gives a supported, attributed, qualification-preserving, correctly timestamped verbatim quote, and no chunk with evidence contradicts it or reports a defect.
- `fail`: a contradiction, wrong attribution, hardened/dropped qualification or wrong timestamp in any chunk; evidence that never supports the claim; or **no evidence in any chunk** (missing evidence can never pass).
- `flag`: one slice calls the claim merely unsupported while another gives clean support (unresolved, needs a human).

Each chunk may also report localized `concerns` (minor → flag, material → fail) and `omitted_topics` (substantive source topics in that slice the summary does not cover). Instead of a per-chunk coverage flag there is one final whole-summary coverage audit call: it receives all candidate omissions plus the full claim list, dismisses topics the summary covers elsewhere (or small talk), and returns `coverage_complete` with the confirmed `omissions`. Any confirmed omission fails the review. Limitation: the audit only sees candidates the chunk reviewers proposed.

`review.md` stores JSON: `video_id`, `summary_sha256`, `verdict`, `chunks`, `claims` (each with `id`, `claim`, `section`, `status`, `supported`, `attributed`, `qualifications_preserved`, `timestamp`, `quote`, `evidence[]` of clean `{chunk,timestamp,quote}`, `issues[]`), `concerns`, `coverage_complete`, `omissions`. Verdict is `fail` for any failed claim, material concern, incomplete coverage or no claims; `flag` for any flagged claim or minor concern; else `pass`. Flag, fail, absent and stale reviews block publication; only a current-hash pass is publishable. `queue_state.completed_review()` separates structurally valid reviews bound to the exact current summary hash from `queue_state.current_review()`, which is strict publication eligibility. Completed fail/flag and incomplete-coverage reviews are retained and skipped on rerun, but malformed or stale records remain queued. Publication re-derives claims and requires a pass, complete coverage, no concerns/issues, verbatim timestamp-matching evidence, and evidence inside each claim's derived section window. Section ends use the nearest strictly later timestamp chronologically; repeated quotes are resolved at the occurrence overlapping the cited timestamp. A standalone validated `Đã gộp vào mục…` editorial cross-reference is omitted from claims; nearby medical assertions remain claims. The downstream validator/consolidation gate must call `queue_state.publishable(video_dir)` before publishing; no publishing path is implemented in this ticket. The review never edits `summary.md`.

Model-based fidelity judgments are not a substitute for human medical or editorial review. The software verifies record structure, evidence quote presence and hash freshness, not semantic entailment; Claude may miss issues. Usage-limit messages stop the batch and report the reset hint while preserving already committed reviews.
